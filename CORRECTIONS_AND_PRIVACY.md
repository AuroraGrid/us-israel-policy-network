# Corrections and Privacy Policy

This project is designed to support accountable public-interest research.

## Corrections

Correction requests should identify:

1. the exact page or claim;
2. the disputed wording;
3. the supporting primary record or reliable source;
4. the requested correction;
5. whether the issue concerns factual accuracy, attribution, context, identity, or privacy.

Submissions are evaluated against the evidence standard used by the project. Material corrections should be documented with the date and reason for the change.

## Privacy

The project avoids publishing unnecessary personal data about private individuals. Association, donation, attendance, employment, communication, or contact does not by itself establish wrongdoing or control.

Sensitive identifying details may be removed, minimized, or withheld when they do not materially advance the public-interest analysis.

## Identity disputes

Where two people may share a name or where identity resolution is uncertain, the record should remain qualified until supported by reliable identifying information.

## No suppression guarantee

A request does not guarantee removal. Public records and matters of legitimate public interest may remain, but factual errors, misleading context, unnecessary private details, and mistaken identities should be corrected when established.
