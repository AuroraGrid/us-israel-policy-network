SECTION I

Scope, language, and evidentiary rules

This report examines institutions and documented mechanisms. It does not assign collective responsibility to Jewish people, Israelis, Americans, or any religious or ethnic group.

Judaism, Israeli citizenship, Zionism, pro-Israel advocacy, the Israeli state, and specific lobbying organizations are distinct categories. Claims are evaluated at the level of the named actor and cited record.

Record labels

VERIFIED FACT
Primary or authoritative records directly support the statement.
SUPPORTED
Strong reporting or multiple independent sources support it.
INFERENCE
A reasoned conclusion extending beyond source text.
UNRESOLVED
Evidence or attribution remains incomplete or disputed.
NOT PROVEN
The available record does not establish the claim.

SECTION III

Security architecture and regional diplomacy

2016–2028

Ten-year security-assistance framework

The current memorandum framework provides $3.8 billion annually in security assistance, subject to congressional appropriations.

2020

Abraham Accords

Israel normalized relations with the UAE and Bahrain, with later arrangements involving Morocco and Sudan. The agreements expanded diplomatic and commercial ties while leaving the Israeli–Palestinian conflict unresolved.

2023–2025

Wartime expansion

CRS records accelerated deliveries, supplemental appropriations, and additional arms-sale notifications after October 7, 2023. Congressional support remained strong while opposition to unconditional assistance increased.

SECTION IV

Intelligence and surveillance controversies

CASE 01UNRESOLVED

USS Liberty, 1967

Israeli forces attacked the U.S. Navy ship, killing 34 and wounding 171. Israel apologized and said the ship was misidentified. Whether the attack was deliberate remains contested.

Constraint: Attack and casualties are verified; intent is not conclusively resolved.

CASE 02VERIFIED

Commercial spyware risk

The U.S. government restricted use of commercial spyware posing counterintelligence, security, or human-rights risks. Commerce placed NSO Group and Candiru on the Entity List in 2021.

Constraint: Attribution must be made incident by incident.

CASE 03SUPPORTED

Targeting of U.S. personnel

Officials disclosed that at least 50 U.S. government personnel in at least 10 countries had been confirmed or suspected targets of commercial spyware.

Constraint: The public record does not identify every tool, customer, or responsible government.

SECTION V

Claim ledger

Israeli initiation of the June 1967 warVERIFIED

Israel initiated the war with a preemptive strike. Whether the strike was necessary to prevent an imminent attack remains disputed.

Qatari transfers into Hamas-governed GazaSUPPORTED

Israel approved Qatari transfers that supported civil administration, poor families, fuel, and humanitarian needs. Critics argue the policy indirectly stabilized Hamas rule; Israeli officials defended it as a mechanism to reduce humanitarian deterioration and escalation.

White House cellular surveillance devicesUNRESOLVED

Politico reported that former U.S. officials assessed Israel was likely responsible. Israel denied the allegation, and no public prosecution established responsibility.

A single hidden network controls U.S. policyNOT PROVEN

The record establishes substantial influence by multiple actors. It does not establish unified command, total control, or a covert ethnic or religious apparatus.
